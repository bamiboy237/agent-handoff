Questions

Which agents work?
OpenCode, Claude Code, Codex, Pi, and any agent that supports MCP.
Where does it run?
Daytona, E2B, Vercel, Prime Intellect, or your own machine over SSH.
How do I get the work back?
As a draft pull request.
What happens to my uncommitted changes?
They’re copied to the cloud along with your commits, so the agent continues from exactly where you left off.
Can I check on it?
Yes. Your agent gives you a link to watch it live from your phone and send it messages.
